Markets Today: Oct 12

By The Ledger Daily desk · Updated 09:40 UTC

Equities opened mixed on Monday as traders weighed fresh inflation data against a run of upbeat earnings from chipmakers. The benchmark index was up 0.3% in early trading, led by semiconductor names.

Crypto markets were quiet. Bitcoin held within a narrow range overnight while layer-1 tokens traded sideways ahead of this week's rate decision. Stablecoin volumes on newer chains continued to climb.
